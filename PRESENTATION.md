# AI Instagram Follow Agent
### How Apateu Uses AI to Grow Our Audience

---

## The Problem

Growing Instagram manually is slow and repetitive:
- Finding potential followers takes hours
- Following/unfollowing one by one is tedious
- Easy to miss the right audience

---

## Our Solution

An AI agent that handles Instagram growth **automatically** — from finding the right people to following and unfollowing them.

---

## How It Works

### 1. We Pick a Target
We give the agent a competitor's account or a relevant post.
> *"Find everyone who follows @competitor"*

### 2. Agent Scrapes Instagram
The agent opens Instagram like a real user, scrolls through the follower list, and collects all the usernames automatically.

### 3. Agent Follows Them
The agent visits each profile and follows them — one by one, with human-like pauses and mouse movements so it doesn't look like a bot.

### 4. Agent Unfollows Non-Followers
After a few days, the agent sweeps through and unfollows anyone who didn't follow back — keeping our following list clean.

---

## Why It Matters

- Targets **people already interested** in similar content
- Runs in the background — **zero manual effort**
- Grows the account **consistently every day**
- Handles multiple accounts at once

---

## The Result

**Automated, targeted audience growth** — so our team can focus on content instead of clicking buttons.
